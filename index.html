About & Visiting Info

North Hall Park is a 123-acre Hall County park and recreation complex in the Appalachian foothills north of Gainesville. Alongside its ballfields, walking track, and community center, the park's multi-use hiking trails wind through mixed hardwood forest, edge habitat, and wetland areas \u2014 home to the plants, trees, and animals featured on this map.
